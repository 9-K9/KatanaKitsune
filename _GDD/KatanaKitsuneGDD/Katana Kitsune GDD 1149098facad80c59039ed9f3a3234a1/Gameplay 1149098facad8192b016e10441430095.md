# Core Gameplay

Katana Kitsune is an isometric roguelike that combines elements of Japanese folklore with challenging, procedurally generated gameplay. Players control a kitsune (fox spirit) armed with a magical katana, navigating through ever-changing landscapes filled with yokai and other mythical threats.

Core gameplay elements include:

- **Isometric Hex-Based World:** The game presents a unique isometric view with a hex-based grid system. This design combines 2D and 3D aesthetics, offering strategic depth for positioning and movement. The hex layout enhances tactical decision-making in both exploration and combat.
- **Yokai Magic Deck-Building Combat:** Battles unfold through a strategic, turn-based system featuring yokai-inspired magic cards. Players build and manage a deck of magical abilities, each representing different yokai powers. During combat, players draw and play these cards, unleashing supernatural effects. Enemies use their own yokai abilities, creating dynamic and unpredictable encounters.
- **Roguelike Elements:** Each playthrough offers a unique experience with procedurally generated levels, randomized enemy placements, and permadeath. Players must adapt their strategies to overcome new challenges in each run.
- **Dynamic Combat:** The combat system focuses on tactical decision-making, combining swift katana strikes with fox magic. Players must carefully manage their resources and positioning to survive encounters.
- **Ability Progression:** As players progress, they unlock and upgrade various kitsune abilities, such as shapeshifting, illusion creation, and elemental manipulation. These powers can be combined in creative ways to overcome obstacles and defeat enemies.
- **Environmental Interaction:** The isometric layout allows for complex level designs with interactive elements. Players can use the environment to their advantage, solving puzzles and creating tactical opportunities in combat.
- **Loot and Upgrades:** Defeating enemies and exploring the procedurally generated worlds yields various items, weapons, and artifacts. Players must make strategic choices about which items to keep and how to build their character for each run.

The core gameplay loop involves exploring randomly generated levels, engaging in tactical combat, collecting loot and upgrades, and progressively unlocking new abilities and story elements. The roguelike nature ensures high replayability, with each run providing a fresh challenge and opportunity for players to master the game's systems.

# Game Mechanics

Key game mechanics in Katana Kitsune include:

- **Hex-Based Movement:** Players navigate through an isometric world using a hex grid system, allowing for strategic positioning and movement.
- **Yokai Magic Deck-Building:** Combat revolves around building and using a deck of yokai-inspired magic cards, each representing different supernatural abilities.
- **Procedural Generation:** Levels, enemy placements, and loot are randomly generated for each playthrough, ensuring a unique experience every time.
- **Permadeath:** In true roguelike fashion, death results in starting over from the beginning, with some progression elements carrying over.
- **Katana Combat:** Close-quarters combat using a magical katana, combined with fox magic for ranged and area-of-effect attacks.
- **Kitsune Abilities:** Players can unlock and upgrade various fox spirit powers, including shapeshifting and elemental manipulation.
- **Environmental Interaction:** The game world features interactive elements that can be used for puzzle-solving and tactical advantage in combat.
- **Loot and Upgrade System:** Players collect items, weapons, and artifacts to enhance their character's abilities and combat effectiveness.

# Progressions

## Game Progressions

- **Tutorial Area:** Introduces basic mechanics, movement, and combat in a controlled environment.
- **Enchanted Forest:** First main area with increasing difficulty, introducing basic yokai enemies and environmental hazards.
- **Abandoned Village:** More complex level layouts, new enemy types, and basic puzzle elements.
- **Misty Mountains:** Introduces vertical level design and flying enemies, testing player's mastery of movement and combat.
- **Cursed Shrine:** Complex puzzle-based area with challenging enemy encounters and mini-boss fights.
- **Spirit Realm:** Final area with the most difficult enemies, complex level designs, and culminating in the final boss battle.

Each area becomes progressively more challenging, introducing new mechanics, enemies, and environmental hazards. The procedural generation ensures that while the overall difficulty curve remains consistent, the specific layouts and challenges vary with each playthrough.

## Character Progressions

Character progression in Katana Kitsune is designed to complement the game's roguelike nature while providing a sense of long-term advancement. Key aspects include:

- **Kitsune Tails:** Players start with one tail and can unlock up to nine, each granting new abilities or enhancing existing ones.
- **Yokai Card Collection:** Defeating enemies and exploring levels allows players to collect new yokai cards, expanding their magical arsenal.
- **Skill Trees:** Each tail unlocks a unique skill tree, allowing players to specialize in different playstyles (e.g., illusion, elemental manipulation, shapeshifting).
- **Katana Upgrades:** Players can enhance their magical katana, unlocking new combat techniques and increasing its power.
- **Permanent Upgrades:** Certain achievements or milestones unlock permanent upgrades that persist across runs, such as increased health or starting resources.
- **Rune System:** Collectible runes can be equipped to modify abilities or grant passive bonuses, allowing for further character customization.

This progression system ensures that while each run is unique, players feel a sense of advancement and increasing power over multiple playthroughs.

# Obstacles & Failure Conditions

Katana Kitsune presents players with various obstacles and challenges throughout their journey. These obstacles test the player's skills, strategy, and adaptability. Failure conditions are designed to be fair but challenging, encouraging players to learn and improve with each attempt.

## Obstacles

- **Yokai Enemies:** Various types of supernatural creatures with unique abilities and attack patterns.
- **Environmental Hazards:** Traps, pitfalls, and dangerous terrain that players must navigate carefully.
- **Puzzle Elements:** Complex mechanisms or magical seals that require problem-solving skills to progress.
- **Resource Management:** Limited health, energy, or magical resources that players must manage efficiently.
- **Time Constraints:** Certain areas or challenges may have time limits, adding pressure to decision-making.
- **Boss Encounters:** Powerful yokai that serve as major obstacles, requiring mastery of game mechanics to overcome.

## Failure Conditions

- **Health Depletion:** When the player's health reaches zero, resulting in death and a game over.
- **Time Expiration:** Failing to complete time-sensitive objectives within the given limit.
- **Falling into Hazards:** Instant death from falling into certain environmental traps or off the map.
- **Corruption Overload:** Accumulating too much yokai corruption, leading to the kitsune losing control.
- **Failed Objectives:** Not meeting specific mission requirements in certain scenarios.
- **Deck Depletion:** Running out of yokai cards in the middle of a battle, leaving the player vulnerable.

Upon failure, players restart from the beginning of the game or the last checkpoint, depending on the game mode. However, they retain any permanent upgrades or unlocked abilities, allowing for gradual progression and improvement over multiple runs.

# Level Designs

The level design for Katana Kitsune in the Godot game engine will focus on creating an engaging, procedurally generated 2D isometric world that balances the roguelite and deck-building elements. Here's a detailed explanation of the level design approach:

## Overworld Structure

The game world will be divided into interconnected areas, following a structure of town-dungeon-town:

- **Towns:** Safe zones where players can rest, trade, and prepare for their next adventure.
- **Dungeons:** Procedurally generated areas filled with enemies, traps, and treasures.
- **Transition Areas:** Smaller, less challenging zones connecting towns and dungeons.

## Isometric Grid System

We'll use Godot's 2D engine with an isometric tileset to create the game's distinctive look:

- **Tile-based Design:** Create a custom TileMap node with isometric tiles for easy level construction.
- **Hex-based Movement:** Implement a hex-grid overlay for character movement and positioning.
- **Z-indexing:** Utilize Godot's z-index property to manage depth and create a sense of 3D space in the 2D environment.

## Procedural Generation

To create varied and replayable levels, we'll implement a procedural generation system:

- **Room-based Approach:** Generate levels by combining pre-designed room templates.
- **Cellular Automata:** Use cellular automata algorithms to create organic-looking terrain and pathways.
- **Constraint-based Generation:** Ensure levels are always completable and adhere to difficulty curves.

## Level Components

Each level will consist of various components to create engaging gameplay:

- **Combat Arenas:** Open areas designed for tactical battles, utilizing the hex-grid system.
- **Puzzle Rooms:** Sections with environmental puzzles that test player's problem-solving skills.
- **Treasure Rooms:** Hidden or locked areas containing valuable loot and new deck cards.
- **Safe Zones:** Small areas where players can rest and strategize between encounters.
- **Boss Arenas:** Larger, more complex rooms designed for challenging boss encounters.

## Environmental Storytelling

Use level design to convey the game's story and atmosphere:

- **Themed Tilesets:** Create distinct visual themes for different areas (e.g., haunted forests, abandoned shrines).
- **Interactive Objects:** Implement clickable objects that reveal lore or trigger events.
- **Dynamic Elements:** Add animated tiles and particles to bring the environment to life.

## Technical Implementation

Utilize Godot's features for efficient level design and rendering:

- **Scene Instancing:** Use instanced scenes for recurring elements like enemies and interactive objects.
- **YSort Nodes:** Implement YSort nodes to automatically manage rendering order of isometric elements.
- **Navigation2D:** Use Navigation2D nodes for pathfinding in the hex-based grid system.
- **Shaders:** Develop custom shaders for special effects like fog of war or magical auras.

By combining these elements, we can create a rich, varied, and engaging world for Katana Kitsune that supports both the roguelite gameplay and the deck-building combat system, all while maintaining the charm and style of traditional Japanese RPGs.
