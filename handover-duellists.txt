THREE FINDINGS THE API DOES NOT TELL YOU

  1. A custom v3 clip costs 48 generations and takes ~8 MINUTES for eight
     directions. The hint says 30-60s per direction; it is wrong. Templates
     cost 8 generations, run far quicker, and have no pop at all, because they
     pose a skeleton rather than redrawing the character each frame.

  2. Frames come back 216x216, NOT the 160 requested — the silhouette grows the
     canvas. Convenient against the 224 target cells, but any export code
     written for 160 is wrong before it runs.

  3. Only 10 concurrent job slots exist and an 8-direction animation needs 8.
     So: one animation at a time, and whoever else is working MUST NOT queue
     PixelLab jobs or the two of you starve each other.
