# Katana Kitsune GDD

> kitsune, **trickster foxes from traditional Japanese folklore**. They are a type of yōkai, a class of supernatural creatures with godlike powers, often equated to the English ghoul or demon. Kitsune are noted for their paranormal abilities, particularly metamorphosis. As they age, these abilities can become more powerful.

### High-level Details

**Brief Story:**

In a mystical feudal Japan, Akane, a young kitsune girl, lives peacefully with her fox clan. One fateful night, a group of corrupt samurai, led by a powerful daimyo, massacres her entire clan in a bid to harness their supernatural powers. Barely escaping with her life, Akane is consumed by grief and rage. She vows to master the ancient arts of both kitsune magic and samurai swordsmanship, transforming herself into a deadly force of vengeance.

As Akane embarks on her quest for retribution, she discovers a darker plot involving demonic yokai and corrupt human lords. Her journey leads her to make difficult choices, blurring the lines between justice and revenge. As she grows in power, Akane must grapple with her own nature, deciding whether to succumb to the darkness within or find a path to redemption while still avenging her fallen clan.

**Main Objective:**

Navigate through a mystical feudal Japan, mastering kitsune magic and samurai swordsmanship, to avenge Akane's fallen clan and uncover the truth behind the dark plot involving yokai and corrupt lords. Along the journey, players must balance Akane's quest for vengeance with moral choices that will shape her destiny and determine whether she succumbs to darkness or finds redemption.

**Gameplay Pillars:**

- **Dynamic Combat System:** Blend fast-paced katana swordsmanship with mystical kitsune abilities, allowing players to seamlessly switch between melee and magical attacks.
- **Moral Choice Mechanics:** Present players with decisions that affect Akane's path, influencing both gameplay and story outcomes, balancing vengeance and redemption.
- **Evolving Abilities:** Implement a progression system where Akane's kitsune powers and samurai skills grow stronger, unlocking new combat techniques and magical abilities.
- **Mythical World Exploration:** Create a rich, atmospheric feudal Japan filled with supernatural elements, encouraging exploration and discovery of Japanese folklore.
- **Strategic Roguelite Elements:** Incorporate procedurally generated levels and permadeath, with each run providing opportunities to unlock permanent upgrades and uncover more of the story.
